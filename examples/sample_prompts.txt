# Sample Prompts for Tissue Engineering Assets

## Scaffolds & Biomaterials
- a tissue engineering scaffold, porous PCL structure, biodegradable, interconnected pores, 100-300 micron pore size, isometric view
- a hydrogel scaffold for cartilage repair, gelatin methacryloyl, photorealistic, translucent, cellularized
- a 3D printed beta-TCP bone scaffold, interconnected channels, osteoconductive, medical grade
- a decellularized ECM scaffold, native tissue architecture preserved, acellular, regenerative medicine
- a electrospun nanofiber mat for wound healing, aligned fibers, biodegradable polymer

## Bioreactors & Culture Systems
- a perfusion bioreactor for tissue engineering, glass vessel, tubing, sensors, sterile, laboratory setting
- a rotating wall vessel bioreactor, NASA design, microgravity simulation, cell culture
- a microfluidic organ-on-chip device, PDMS, endothelial cells, perfusable channels, microscope view
- a stirred-tank bioreactor for MSC expansion, stainless steel, impeller, sparger, control system

## Cells & Cellular Constructs
- mesenchymal stem cells on a scaffold, confocal microscopy, fluorescent staining, 3D reconstruction

- osteoblasts mineralizing a calcium phosphate scaffold, bone nodule formation, SEM image

## Clinical & Surgical
- a surgical implantation procedure, tissue engineered construct, operating room, sterile

- a cartilage defect repair with MACI, arthroscopic view, fibrin glue, scaffold integration
